AI, it’s time to deliver!

Here’s my take today: the AI race is entering a phase where it has to deliver. Markets want returns on investment. Users want to know whether AI can get things done.

According to the Financial Times, stocks tied to data center construction have lost a combined $4.2 trillion in market value. Expectations for expanding computing capacity are being reassessed.

A similar shift is happening in applications. Nvidia’s SkillEvaluator framework can check quality and identify overlapping skills, helping teams answer one question: do new skills actually improve task performance?

Meanwhile, the AI calling feature in Meta’s Muse faces a more practical problem: it can contact businesses and make reservations, but some businesses simply hang up. A smooth conversation is only the first step toward getting the job done.

So, as we watch what comes next for AI, task completion rates, cost per successful outcome, and returns on investment will matter more and more.
