AI is getting cheaper, but investing is getting harder. Today’s key takeaway: usage costs are falling, but supply and funding risks remain.

But look at computing power, and the picture gets more complicated. Alibaba shares reportedly fell 7% amid fears of tighter scrutiny of advanced chips. Keep in mind: the available information does not show that new restrictions have taken effect. The stock move reflects concern about that risk.

Funding is another wild card: U.S. October CPI reportedly came in above expectations, and Fed official Hammack is calling for tighter policy. That could dampen expectations for rate cuts.

So lower model prices are worth watching. Investment returns still hinge on two things: whether chips can be delivered, and when spending on computing power turns into revenue.
