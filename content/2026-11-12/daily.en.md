# Personal Daily Brief | November 12, 2026

## Today's Take

> Today's Take: AI competition is expanding beyond capability breakthroughs to unit costs, deployment options and product delivery. Engineering efficiency will become a key differentiator among platforms. Meanwhile, inflation, tariff threats and uncertainty over chip supply and corporate governance are heightening markets' sensitivity to risk. Over the coming weeks, technology asset valuations will hinge on whether technical cost savings translate into revenue and cash flow, and whether investors are willing to bear these uncertainties.

### ByteDance's fast video model doubles speed and cuts prices by about a third

**Event:** Volcano Engine has launched Seedance 2.5 flash, claiming twice the generation speed and prices about 34% lower. Generating 720p video costs about RMB 0.99 per second, with clips up to 30 seconds long. The API will launch on November 15.

**Why it matters:** Lower unit costs and shorter waits make video generation better suited to frequent experimentation and batch production. At the listed price, a 30-second 720p video costs about RMB 29.7. Actual budgets still depend on regeneration attempts, the proportion of usable output and the quality gap between the fast and flagship versions.

### Open-source TensorFold inference engine supports three GPU backends

**Event:** TensorFold, developed by ashhart, implements large language model inference in Zig and supports Metal, CUDA and Vulkan. The summary lists 1821 stars and 386 forks.

**Why it matters:** Supporting three GPU interfaces could reduce dependence on a single hardware ecosystem for inference deployment. Engineering teams still need to verify model coverage, operator completeness, GPU memory use and actual throughput. Repository popularity alone does not establish performance or stability.
