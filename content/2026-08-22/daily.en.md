# Personal Daily Report | August 22, 2026

## Today's Take

> Today's Take: What is truly changing is not the volume of news, but how policy, capital, and technology are simultaneously reshaping expectations. High debt, elevated interest rates, and tariff shocks are constraining growth, while the rebound in risk assets has not removed the pressure from interest rates. Meanwhile, AI competition is shifting toward model licensing, visual understanding, and tool execution. Apple’s bet on lightweight wearables and Nvidia’s pursuit of model assets show that access points, chips, and talent are being repriced.

### Apple Scales Back VR Development to Focus on Siri and Smart Glasses

**Event:** Apple has disbanded its VR development team and cut at least 60 technical positions. Over the past six months, it has redirected resources toward Siri AI and lightweight AR glasses while working with optical manufacturers on waveguide display technology.

**Why it matters:** This resource shift changes Apple’s priorities for the next generation of computing interfaces: bulky headsets are giving way to AI assistants and glasses. The outlook now depends on waveguide manufacturing yields, battery life, and whether integration with the iPhone can support a consumer-grade product.
