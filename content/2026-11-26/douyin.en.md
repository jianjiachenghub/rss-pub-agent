Funding AI is getting more expensive.

Today’s key takeaway: AI investment faces a cash flow test, and markets increasingly want to know when all that spending will pay off.

SoftBank’s credit risk premium reportedly topped 500 basis points for the first time in roughly four years. Investors are demanding more compensation for risk, which could push up its future borrowing costs.

And debt-fueled expansion could get even bigger. JPMorgan expects companies to raise $450 billion through investment-grade bonds in 2027 to fund AI capital spending. Whether those investments generate enough cash flow will directly affect the pressure to repay that debt.

Meanwhile, tech stocks have sold off, while software stocks have held up relatively well. That sends a signal: markets are paying closer attention to whether AI can deliver revenue.

So, when watching AI, look beyond models and chips and ask three questions: Are customers paying? Is that revenue turning into cash? Can the money earned cover borrowing costs?
