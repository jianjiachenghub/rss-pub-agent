# Today’s Must-Read Stories Go Beyond AI

August 21 brought a flood of news, but these five themes are the ones most likely to shape what comes next for markets and the tech industry:

### 1️⃣ AI models are competing to “see and act”

DeepSeek unveiled an experimental vision model aimed at rivaling Claude Opus 4.8. Tesla’s in-car systems in China are also beginning to divide tasks between Doubao and DeepSeek.

The signal is clear: model competition is shifting from “who can chat better” to who can operate in real-world settings involving images, documents, cars, and more.

### 2️⃣ The key to AI agents isn’t using more—it’s using them well

OpenAI open-sourced Codex Harness, making it easier for companies to build task execution, memory, tool use, and human approval into their products.

But Meituan’s review of its company-wide “raising shrimp” initiative offered a harsh reality check: 80,000 employees consumed tens of millions of yuan in computing power every day without achieving a comparable increase in productivity.

What AI adoption may truly lack, then, isn’t computing power but redesigned workflows, permission management, and outcome evaluation.

### 3️⃣ The AI boom is facing a commercial reality check

OpenAI plans to go public by 2027 at the latest, while Anthropic is targeting a record-breaking IPO. Meanwhile, AI suppliers have already begun preparing for a potential pullback in data-center investment.

Samsung’s plan to spend up to roughly $80 billion on dividends and share buybacks also suggests that profits from the memory-chip industry are increasingly flowing to shareholders rather than capacity expansion.

Markets will no longer ask only how powerful the models are. They will also demand answers: How much revenue is there? How high are margins? Can the cash burn continue?

### 4️⃣ The dollar, bonds, gold, and Bitcoin are all moving at once

Bond-market intervention and fiscal concerns weighed on the dollar, while gold and Bitcoin posted a rare simultaneous rise, with Bitcoin briefly nearing $80,000.

Inflation is heating up in Japan, the UK and France face fiscal pressure, and talk of “selling America” is growing.

The main driver of this market move may be more than improving risk appetite—it may also reflect a repricing of fiscal credibility, interest rates, and dollar-denominated assets.

### 5️⃣ Algorithms are beginning to carry real legal liability

Uber could face an €825 million fine in the Netherlands for automatically suspending drivers’ accounts, while AI-powered scams are beginning to target private-capital clients.

As companies adopt agents, the focus has shifted from “Can we use them?” to:

Who authorizes them? What can they access? Can problems be traced? Are critical decisions reviewed by humans?

📌 Today in one sentence:

AI is moving beyond the technology race and into real-world tests of business, organizations, and regulation. At the same time, the most important variables in global markets are shifting from growth expectations toward fiscal credibility and financing costs.
