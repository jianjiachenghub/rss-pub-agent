Today’s most important takeaway: the AI industry has moved beyond “who has the strongest model” to “who can deploy AI in the real world—and make money from it.”

Three signals make this clear.

First, DeepSeek’s new test model adds visual understanding, with performance reportedly close to Claude Opus 4.8. Tesla China is also using Doubao for navigation and vehicle-control queries, while DeepSeek handles search and casual conversation. Chinese models are moving into high-frequency gateways like cars, images, and documents.

Second, OpenAI has open-sourced Codex Harness, giving developers direct access to agent capabilities such as task execution, tool use, and human approval. The next major interface may no longer be a chatbox, but an AI workspace embedded directly into business workflows.

But the third signal is the biggest warning: Meituan gave 80,000 employees unlimited access to AI agents, burning through tens of millions of yuan in computing costs every day—without a corresponding boost in productivity.

So in the next phase, what’s truly scarce won’t be more tokens or simply bigger models. It will be clearly defined use cases, controlled permissions, and measurable returns on investment. The real AI elimination round is only just beginning.
