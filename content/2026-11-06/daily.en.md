# Personal Daily Brief | November 6, 2026

## Today's Take

> AI assets face growing valuation constraints, with policy and liquidity temporarily outweighing the technology narrative. Chip export policies, scrutiny of infrastructure lending, expectations of rate hikes in Japan, and the unwinding of crowded positions could amplify valuation swings. The rotation into consumer and internet stocks is worth tracking but does not yet confirm a trend reversal. Advances in models, products, and developer tools still need to deliver revenue and engineering efficiency gains to justify the capital invested.

## Watch Signals

Watch three groups of signals: whether chip export policies and reviews of data center lending lead to substantive restrictions; whether expectations of rate hikes in Japan, the unwinding of crowded trades, and capital rotation persist; and whether platform companies and chipmakers adjust capital spending, while advances in model capabilities, changes in product formats, and upgraded developer tools improve paid adoption and efficiency. These signals will help distinguish a short-term valuation correction from a slowdown in industry investment.
