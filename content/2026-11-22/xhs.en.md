**11.22 Daily Brief｜Compute Price Hikes, Stock Market Support, Climate Deadlock**

Today’s stories span tech, finance, housing, and energy. Here are 7 to get you up to speed👇

1️⃣ **AI server prices can rise—even after contracts are signed**
According to the Financial Times, some suppliers have raised prices months after signing contracts, with increases of up to 45%. Meanwhile, Nscale, which is preparing to go public, has slowed hiring and cut spending.
👉 For companies buying computing capacity, locking in budgets and managing cash runway are becoming increasingly important.

2️⃣ **Taiwan and South Korea prepare to support stock markets**
Taiwan is considering lowering margin requirements for stock purchases; South Korea has announced a phased response to volatility and put its stock market stabilization fund on standby.
👉 Watch when these measures take effect and whether money actually enters the market. Lower margin requirements can boost buying power, but also increase leverage risk.

3️⃣ **Housing pressures are shaping interest rate and tax policy**
Rising US mortgage rates are making home financing more expensive; Spain plans to extend its bank windfall tax and use the proceeds for housing investment.
👉 One affects the cost of buying a home; the other addresses how housing is funded. Both deserve continued attention.

4️⃣ **Climate talks stall over the future of fossil fuels**
This round of UN climate talks broke down over disagreements, producing no binding agreement. The two related reports in the daily brief cover the same underlying issue.
👉 A shared path for the energy transition is becoming harder to coordinate. Businesses still need to track carbon policies already in force in each market.

5️⃣ **Demand is taking center stage in metals pricing**
Metals markets saw both tight supplies and sharp price drops in a single week, while participants at a copper industry gathering in Shanghai were downbeat about demand.
👉 How long “shortages” can support prices depends on inventories, spot trading, and end-user orders.

6️⃣ **Medical AI is entering doctors’ workflows**
Ant’s Afu for Doctors has integrated UpToDate, giving doctors across China free access to the medical knowledge base on phones and computers through December 31, 2026. Ant also disclosed that Afu has 150 million users, 55% from tier-three cities and below.
👉 Demand at scale is already evident. The next things to watch are citation accuracy, service outcomes, and coordination with referral services.

7️⃣ **AI features are advancing, and usage rules are getting more specific**
Tesla has begun rolling out FSD V15, adding new ways to visualize and interact with its driving plans; Bilibili is seeking feedback on AI agent account governance, proposing AI identity labels and limits on posting frequency.
👉 When drivers need to take over, and how to handle comment spam, both matter to the user experience.
