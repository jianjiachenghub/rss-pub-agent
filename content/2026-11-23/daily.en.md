# Personal Daily Brief | November 23, 2026

## Today's Take

> Today's central theme is the divergence between technological progress and risk pricing. AI capabilities and products continue to evolve, with competition expanding from model performance to development efficiency, costs and chip design. Meanwhile, leveraged positions unwinding and financing premiums shrinking expose fragile buying support. Corporate appetite for acquisitions remains strong. The coming weeks will test whether technological gains materialize and platform integration clears cost and regulatory hurdles.

### OpenAI optimizes AMD chip circuitry, with modified designs reaching tape-out

**Event:** OpenAI reportedly used GPT to rewrite RTL code for AMD's flagship GPU, achieving up to a 14% net improvement across power, performance and area metrics in under five months. The modified designs have reached tape-out.

**Why it matters:** The key variable is the cost of chip-design iteration. Models can now optimize mature RTL and carry changes through to tape-out. Silicon measurements and engineering effort still need scrutiny: a PPA improvement of up to 14% does not directly translate into a 14% performance gain for the entire GPU.

### US and UK companies cut currency hedges as unprotected losses rise

**Event:** An industry survey found that US and UK companies reduced currency hedging in the third quarter by the most in over two years, taking hedge volumes to their lowest since 2024. Losses from unhedged currency movements rose at the same time.

**Why it matters:** Lower hedge coverage exposes operating cash flow and earnings to more currency volatility. Companies with large mismatches between the currencies of their revenue and costs will depend more on exchange-rate trends and natural hedges for predictable earnings, potentially widening quarterly profit swings.
