AI is advancing, but money is pulling out. Today’s key takeaway: technological progress doesn’t automatically translate into higher stock prices.

Zhipu has launched a GLM-5.4 coding plan, adding visual input and raising usage limits by 50% for some users. Coding assistants can handle more tasks now, but whether the subscription is worth it still depends on whether they can complete complex tasks—and whether the usage allowance is enough.

A deeper shift: OpenAI has reportedly used GPT to optimize AMD chip circuits, with those designs already sent for fabrication and gains of up to 14% on a combined measure of power, performance, and area. Keep in mind: that doesn’t mean the entire GPU is 14% faster. The results still need to be verified on actual chips.

Meanwhile, Bloomberg reports that margin calls in Taiwan surged 200-fold last week. Investors who can’t put up the cash may be forced to sell stocks, amplifying volatility.

These are the two forces shaping today’s AI market: technology keeps advancing, while leverage is starting to come under pressure. Judge products by real-world efficiency. When watching the market, keep an eye on money flows and forced selling pressure, too.
