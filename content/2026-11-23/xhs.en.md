**11.23｜New Tech, Markets Under Pressure**

There’s more to watch today than model launches. Coding tools are improving, investors are facing margin calls, big companies are pursuing acquisitions, and exchange rates and healthcare policy are affecting corporate profits.

These 6 stories cover the key points👇

💻 **① GLM-5.4 launches: how much work can it do for you?**

Zhipu’s GLM-5.4 is now available through Coding Plan, adding visual input and stronger general-purpose coding capabilities. Usage quotas for V2, V3, and Team users have increased to 1.5 times their previous levels.

For users, the draw is its ability to handle development tasks involving things like interface screenshots. The recurrent architecture described by the company is worth watching, but the real test is practical: can it get complex tasks right, how quickly does it respond, and how much quota does each task consume?

🔧 **② AI-assisted chip design moves to silicon validation**

According to reports, OpenAI used GPT to optimize RTL code for AMD’s flagship GPU. The resulting designs have been sent for fabrication, with a net improvement of up to 14% in the combined power, performance, and area metric.

That 14% refers to a combined design metric. The ultimate gains will depend on measurements from actual silicon and a full accounting of the engineering effort.

📉 **③ AI stock volatility starts squeezing leveraged accounts**

Bloomberg reports that margin call notices in Taiwan surged 200-fold last week.

A margin call means an account’s collateral has fallen short, and the broker requires more funds. If investors can’t pay, forced selling may follow, deepening the decline.

To gauge whether the pressure is easing, outstanding margin loans and the scale of margin calls are more useful indicators than a one-day rebound.

🤝 **④ Stocks are under pressure, but companies are still pursuing big deals**

Amazon has reportedly agreed to acquire most of Decart’s business for $7.5 billion. Reports of CrowdStrike acquiring Zscaler have also drawn attention, though the summary provides no deal value or update on closing.

This suggests companies’ long-term plans can differ from investors’ current appetite for risk. Whether an acquisition pays off ultimately depends on how well the technology, customers, and teams come together.

₿ **⑤ Bitcoin: watch fund flows and financing models**

Reports cited in the daily briefing show continued outflows from Bitcoin ETFs, with Bitcoin giving back most of its gains. Strategy’s market value has fallen below the value of its Bitcoin holdings.

The former signals whether sustained buying demand is there; the latter affects the company’s cycle of issuing shares to fund more Bitcoin purchases.

To judge whether Strategy is cheap, you also need to factor in its debt.

🌍 **⑥ Three developments in the economy and healthcare also deserve attention**

• **US growth slows**: Business activity is still expanding, but at its slowest pace in four months, adding uncertainty to corporate earnings and interest-rate expectations.

• **Companies have less currency protection**: Foreign exchange hedging by US and UK companies has fallen to its lowest level since 2024, leaving cash flow and profits more directly exposed to currency swings.

• **Healthcare policy affects valuations**: The latest US Medicare proposal sent health insurance stocks lower again. Next, watch how the proposal’s details reshape expectations for the industry.

When reading today’s news, ask one more question: **Whose productivity, cash flow, or financing terms does this actually change?** That makes many complex stories easier to understand.
