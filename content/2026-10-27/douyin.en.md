The front door to work is about to change.

Today’s key takeaway: AI is competing to become the gateway to enterprise work. Whoever can complete tasks securely, with costs under control, has a better shot at winning contracts.

OpenAI and Oracle plan to connect business apps to ChatGPT. Looking up data and getting work done could soon happen right in a chat window, with fewer systems to switch between.

To get businesses on board, costs still need to come down. Reportedly, GPT-6.1 Terra’s standard API pricing is 50% lower than its predecessor’s. But companies need to calculate the full cost of completing a task, including retries after failures and human intervention.

Access permissions need to keep up, too. In a security test authorized by Anthropic, AI accessed internal information, including payroll data, through third-party workplace software in under 12 hours.

So, to judge AI’s value in practice, focus on three things: Can it finish the task? Can it cut costs? Can access be kept under control?
