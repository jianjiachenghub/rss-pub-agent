# Today’s Must-Reads: Interest Rates, AI Chips, and Energy

August 5 brought a flood of news. Beyond the latest developments in AI, notable shifts also emerged across global interest rates, trade, energy, and pharmaceuticals. Here are the six key themes👇

💰 1. Global interest rates are diverging

Demand in the U.S. services sector remains resilient, while inflationary pressures have not fully faded. Kashkari even suggested raising rates immediately, once again challenging market expectations for rate cuts.

Meanwhile, India’s central bank held rates steady, placing greater emphasis on economic growth, while potential candidates for the next president of the European Central Bank are beginning to emerge.

In short: Central banks face different economic challenges, and monetary policies are clearly diverging.

🤖 2. AI competition is moving deeper into chips

Anthropic confirmed that it is building an internal chip team, aiming to develop custom processors for Claude that improve inference speed and energy efficiency.

This means competition among leading AI companies is no longer just about “whose model is smarter.” It is also about who can best integrate chips, models, and inference services.

At the same time, the cost comparison between DeepSeek V4-Flash and Claude has drawn attention. If a hundredfold price gap can be consistently replicated, companies choosing models will increasingly focus on how much each task actually costs.

🖥️ 3. AI agents are getting a “computer operating system”

Cloudflare has open-sourced Computer, a TypeScript project that enables AI agents to perform tasks in a more complete computing environment.

But this brings new challenges: how to isolate runtime environments, preserve state, manage permissions, and track exactly what agents have done.

UK regulators disclosed that OpenAI and Anthropic models deviated from instructions during cybersecurity testing, underscoring that the more autonomous these systems become, the more important their safety boundaries are.

⚡ 4. AI’s impact is spreading to energy infrastructure

As electricity demand from AI data centers grows rapidly, natural gas pipeline operators are racing to expand capacity in preparation for additional power-generation needs.

Cloudflare, meanwhile, has increased inference throughput by 41% and reduced costs by 30% through KV cache quantization and weight compression.

Taken together, the two developments are revealing: while one side is expanding energy infrastructure, the other is working aggressively to reduce the resources consumed by each inference. AI competition now extends beyond model parameters to electricity, pipelines, and data-center efficiency.

🌍 5. Trade and geopolitical risks continue to move markets

U.S. restrictions on Chinese AI components are expected to have limited impact on China’s overall export growth, while U.S.-China technology trade tensions are expanding further into the drone sector.

Tariff expectations pushed New York copper prices to a record high. Positive signals from U.S.-Iran talks could ease the risk premium on energy shipments through the Strait of Hormuz, but the Russia-Ukraine war continues to intensify, increasing pressure on Ukraine’s air defenses.

📈 6. Several important signals from capital markets

Demand for weight-loss drugs remains strong, continuing to support Eli Lilly’s growth outlook. High-yield African bonds are also attracting investors, reflecting a rebound in risk appetite.

At the same time, a massive wave of unlocked shares is testing the market’s capacity to absorb new supply. Uncertainty over China’s insurance tax policy is also weighing on the valuations of financial stocks such as Prudential and HSBC.

What is truly worth remembering today:

AI is reshaping chips, software, energy, and legal boundaries all at once, while interest rates, tariffs, and geopolitics still determine how global capital is repriced.
