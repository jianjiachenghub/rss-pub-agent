# Personal Daily Brief | October 29, 2026

## Today's Take

> Today's central view is that AI's expansion is facing a returns test: revenue growth and spending on compute are not yet enough to justify higher valuations. Multi-agent development tools are shifting competition toward the combination of model capabilities and engineering efficiency, while platforms and chip supply chains face pressure to deliver profits. Shifting European interest-rate expectations and energy policies mean appetite for technology assets must also withstand the dual test of funding costs and the inflation outlook.

## Watch Signals

Watch three sets of signals: whether model upgrades and multi-agent tools shorten delivery cycles and generate paying demand; whether money leaving technology stocks moves into assets with more stable cash flows; and whether ECB guidance, the pace of oil releases, and regulatory constraints on chip acquisitions change financing costs and supply expectations. The focus is whether policy, capital flows, and practical technology adoption can reinforce one another.
