AI is burning cash. Banks are getting picky.

Today’s key takeaway: How fast AI infrastructure can expand increasingly depends on financing costs—and whether revenue can keep pace with spending.

Oracle is seeking $20 billion to expand its AI business, while Meta is considering taking on more debt. The giants keep doubling down, but tomorrow’s cash flow has to support today’s borrowing.

The Wall Street Journal reports that lenders are screening borrowers more closely and demanding tougher financing terms. As banks gain leverage, when projects turn a profit and how they’ll repay their loans will matter more directly to whether funding comes through.

Meanwhile, U.S. tech stocks have entered correction territory. Concerns about returns on AI spending are weighing on market sentiment.

To assess the strength of AI companies’ expansion from here, watch financing costs, whether revenue materializes, and cash flow. Those that turn investment into steady cash inflows will be in a stronger position to keep expanding.
