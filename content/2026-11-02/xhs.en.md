11.02 Daily Brief｜7 Changes Worth Watching Today

There’s more than AI news today. Where rates are headed, whether energy risks will ease, and where investors are moving their money all deserve attention👇

① 📉 Stocks: Selling eases, but fund flows remain key
U.S. tech stocks have entered correction territory, while selling pressure on global AI stocks has eased. Next, watch earnings expectations and whether investors keep moving money back in.
In China, policymakers are encouraging institutions to make greater use of two stock market support tools. The key questions: how much fresh capital they bring in, and when it arrives.

② 🏦 UK: Watch this Thursday’s rate decision
A Bloomberg survey of 35 economists points to a Bank of England rate hike. If it happens, it would be the first since 2023.
Remember, this is a survey forecast for now. The actual decision and subsequent comments will shape pound-denominated assets and business borrowing costs.

③ 🛢️ U.S.–Iran talks: A key signal for energy markets
Iran’s president says the supreme leader has given the government greater negotiating authority. Trump has also signaled that a deal could be reached.
Progress in the talks could ease the geopolitical risk premium in energy prices, but the details and follow-through will matter.

④ 🔐 Crypto: Watch withdrawals and reserves, too
Bloomberg reports that Binance has seen $4.7 billion in cumulative net outflows since resuming withdrawals after a hack. Meanwhile, Bitcoin has shown resilience following a major theft.
With prices weathering the shock, watch whether withdrawals keep rising and whether platform reserves can meet demand.

⑤ 💰 AI infrastructure: Financing conditions are starting to shape expansion
Oracle is seeking $20 billion in funding, Meta is considering taking on more debt, and Singapore-based data center operator STT GDC is also in loan talks.
Meanwhile, Wall Street is becoming more selective about borrowers. How quickly projects get built increasingly depends on borrowing costs, repayment terms, and how soon revenue materializes.

⑥ 🖥️ Desktop agents: Watch how reliably they operate
Vercel’s Agent Desktop reads interfaces through the operating system’s accessibility tree, providing stable element references and safe retries.
In plain English, it helps agents find buttons more reliably and recover from failed actions. How well it works still depends on each app’s support for accessibility interfaces.

⑦ 🌐 AI crawling: Website opt-out rules are becoming standardized
Anthropic has already adopted a standardized robots.txt opt-out signal. OpenAI plans to implement it within months, while Google and Microsoft are expected to follow next year.
For websites, this could reduce the work of maintaining separate rules for each company. The ultimate impact will depend on how participating companies follow through.
