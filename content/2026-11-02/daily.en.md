# Personal Daily Report | November 2, 2026

## Today's Take

> AI expansion is facing simultaneous tests of engineering reliability and financing discipline. Turning model capabilities into usable enterprise products increasingly depends on developer tools, chip supply and supply-chain coordination. Rising financing needs and tighter borrower screening are testing whether funding can keep pace with enthusiasm for the technology; shifts in interest rates, geopolitical negotiations and data-use rules could reset the returns required to justify expansion.

### Four Major AI Companies Advance a Unified Opt-Out Standard for Training Crawlers

**Event:** Anthropic is the first to adopt a unified robots.txt opt-out signal. OpenAI plans to implement it within months, with Google and Microsoft expected to follow next year. Websites can use the signal to reject crawling for AI training.

**Why it matters:** A unified signal could reduce publishers' costs of maintaining separate crawler rules for each company. Its impact on data availability will depend on the scope and timing of each company's implementation; the standard relies on participants complying.
