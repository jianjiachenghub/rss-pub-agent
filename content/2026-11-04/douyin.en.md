AI orders are booming. The risks are rising too.

Today’s key takeaway: demand for computing power remains strong, but policy is affecting whether orders turn into revenue and projects come online.

AMD’s sales guidance beat market expectations, pointing to strong data center demand. But turning orders into revenue depends on more than delivery.

According to the Financial Times, Oracle canceled a $7 billion AI deal with Tencent under pressure from Trump. Politics is directly affecting business.

Shifts in construction policy are also affecting financing. After candidates who supported slowing data center construction won office, some CoreWeave bonds fell to record lows. The market is starting to worry: project delays could squeeze cash inflows and make debt harder to repay. Still, it remains to be seen whether specific restrictions will take effect.

So as you track AI companies, watch whether orders turn into revenue, whether projects get delivered, and whether growth generates profits and cash flow.
