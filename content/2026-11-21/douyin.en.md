The AI boom has reached its moment of reckoning.

Today's key takeaway: AI investment is facing a test—just how much return can all that spending deliver?

Anthropic's shares have fallen sharply since its IPO, and The Wall Street Journal reports that roughly $4 trillion in market value has been wiped off AI stocks on Wall Street. With valuations so high, markets are asking whether revenue and profits can keep up.

The cost equation for businesses is getting more complicated, too. Trump says he plans to penalize U.S. companies that build data centers overseas. The measures haven't taken effect yet, but choosing where to build computing infrastructure may mean factoring in policy costs alongside electricity and land.

That makes cutting costs crucial. AWS has launched a distributed caching tool that it says can cut self-hosted inference costs by up to 40%. But that maximum isn't a given—actual savings depend on how much context is repeated and how often the cache hits.

Going forward, when assessing AI companies, look beyond model capabilities and watch two things: can they sustain revenue, and can they make each inference cheaper?
