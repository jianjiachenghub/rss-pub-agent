# Personal Daily Report | November 21, 2026

## Today's Take

Turning model capabilities into product value depends on access to those capabilities, inference costs, and developer productivity; technology platform and chip strategies must also account for policy uncertainty. ### OpenAI's anti-distillation safeguards slash chain-of-thought extraction rates

**Event:** OpenAI says adversarial training, leak detection, API restrictions, and other safeguards reduced the success rate for extracting GPT-6.2 Astra's chain of thought from 54% to 0.2% in internal tests.

**Why it matters:** Complete reasoning traces for training student models are becoming harder to obtain, raising the cost of distillation data and the risk of account bans. The decline applies to extraction success in internal tests; it does not establish that all output imitation or capability transfer has been blocked.

### AWS distributed caching tool cuts self-hosted model costs

**Event:** AWS has introduced a distributed KV caching skill in Agent Toolkit to help coding agents integrate with ElastiCache. It says the skill can cut inference costs by up to 40% and time to first token by up to 80%.

**Why it matters:** The gains come from reusing KV caches for repeated context, reducing redundant computation and initial response times in self-hosted inference. Actual savings depend on cache hit rates, context reuse, and caching service overhead; the maximum figures cannot be applied to every workload.

### Zhenwu V900 set for mass production next quarter with triple the compute

**Event:** Alibaba says the Zhenwu V900 is scheduled for mass production and sale in the first quarter of 2027, offering three times the computing power of its predecessor, the M890, with 216GB of memory and 1200GB/s of chip-to-chip interconnect bandwidth.

**Why it matters:** The production schedule provides a concrete milestone for Alibaba Cloud's in-house computing supply, while memory and interconnect specifications affect its ability to support large models. Whether it can lower training and inference costs per unit will depend on software compatibility, cluster efficiency, and delivery volumes.
