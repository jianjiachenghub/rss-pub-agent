How high can AI valuations go? Today’s key takeaway: the market is starting to ask when massive AI spending will pay off.

U.S. stocks have rebounded only slightly after AI-related concerns wiped out roughly $3.2 trillion in market value. Investors clearly still have doubts.

Companies are crunching the numbers again, too. Microsoft is reportedly phasing out arrangements to rent AI computing capacity from Oracle. But that reflects a shift in how it sources capacity. To judge whether overall demand is weakening, we also need to look at Microsoft’s spending on its own infrastructure and other cloud partnerships.

Next comes a more direct test: Anthropic. It reportedly plans to launch its IPO roadshow next week, targeting a $1.5 trillion valuation. But whether it can secure that valuation depends on what public-market investors are willing to pay.

Taken together, these developments show that AI companies increasingly need financial results to justify their valuations. Beyond how powerful the models are, can orders, revenue, and cash flow keep pace? That’s what to watch next.
