**Nov. 7 Daily Brief｜6 Things Worth Watching Today**

A lot to digest today: stock market volatility, inflation pressure, shifting chip investments, and robots entering real-world settings. Here are the highlights👇

📉 **① U.S. stocks rebounded, but sentiment remains shaky**

According to the Financial Times, AI-related concerns wiped roughly $3.2 trillion off U.S. stocks before a modest rebound. Following the jobs report, the Nasdaq rose 1.09%, while the Dow fell 0.46%. Selling pressure also spread to Asia.

The takeaway: the indices are still diverging, and a modest rebound is not enough to confirm that the market has stabilized.

🏦 **② Interest rates: inflation remains a key variable**

Fed official Mary Daly said further rate hikes may be needed to curb inflation. ECB President Christine Lagarde also said inflation could remain elevated in the near term.

This affects companies’ borrowing costs and stock valuations. But one official’s remarks do not mean the Fed has decided to raise rates.

🏭 **③ Chips: a major expansion is canceled**

Onsemi canceled a semiconductor expansion project in the Czech Republic that had called for up to $2 billion in investment.

The immediate impact is lower expectations for new local capacity and supporting investment. Whether this signals weaker demand depends on the reasons for the cancellation and plans for other projects.

🌏 **④ U.S.–China trade: watch how the agreement is implemented**

Bessent and Greer reportedly expressed concerns about compliance with the U.S.–China trade agreement, but the summary did not identify specific disputed provisions or follow-up measures.

What matters next is whether concrete disputes emerge and lead to actual policy changes. It is too early to infer that new tariffs are coming.

💰 **⑤ AI industry: valuations and compute procurement are shifting**

Microsoft is reportedly phasing out arrangements to rent AI computing capacity from Oracle as it reassesses its cloud partnerships. The immediate effect is on how procurement is allocated; the broader outlook for compute demand also depends on Microsoft’s own infrastructure and other partnerships.

On the funding side, Anthropic reportedly plans to launch its IPO roadshow next week, targeting a $1.5 trillion valuation. OpenAI has reportedly closed $11 billion in funding at a $1.25 trillion valuation.

Keep this distinction clear: **a target valuation, funding negotiations, and a completed funding deal are different stages.**

🤖 **⑥ Robotics: read the 91% success rate alongside the 70%**

An Apple team released the HALO system and its code, using a Qwen model to control a Unitree G1 robot. After training on roughly 300 hours of simulated data, the system was tested in real-world environments that had not been mapped in advance:

- Single-task success rate: 91%
- Combined-task success rate: 70%

The key development is that simulation-based training is being put to the test in real environments. Reliability on multistep tasks, the lack of tactile feedback, and stability during contact-based operations still determine where the system can be used.
