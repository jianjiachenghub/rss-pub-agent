### Apple's HALO robot system achieves 91% single-task success rate

**Event:** An Apple team released its HALO system and code, using a Qwen model for hierarchical control of the Unitree G1. Trained on roughly 300 hours of simulated data, the system achieved a 91% success rate on individual tasks and 70% on combined tasks in real-world environments without prebuilt maps.

**Why it matters:** Local execution and simulation-based training could reduce dependence on connectivity and the cost of manual data collection. The 70% success rate on combined tasks shows that multistep execution still needs greater reliability. The lack of tactile sensing and the stability of tasks involving physical contact are key to assessing deployment limits.
