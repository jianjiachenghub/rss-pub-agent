AI stocks are down. Does that mean AI is failing?

Today’s key takeaway: AI adoption is still moving forward, but markets increasingly want to know when all that spending will pay off.

AI stocks are selling off, and the Nasdaq 100 risks entering a bear market. Investors’ patience with heavy spending and distant returns is being tested.

But look at Dell: driven by demand for AI servers, it has raised its full-year sales forecast again. The hardware business is still growing. Stock market sentiment and actual demand are diverging.

Technical barriers are falling, too. Tencent has open-sourced the quantized weights and code for Hy4 flash, allowing a 224-billion-parameter model to run on a Mac mini with 64GB of memory. But whether it’s practical to use—and can achieve widespread adoption—still needs to be proven.

Taken together, these stories show that technical progress and growing orders don’t automatically translate into higher stock prices. What matters next is whether those orders turn into profits, and whether lower costs lead to more real-world use.
