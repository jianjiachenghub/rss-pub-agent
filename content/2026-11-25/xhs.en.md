11.25｜Lighter Models, Markets Under Pressure

A packed news day. Here are the five threads worth watching: tech advances, stock market swings, interest rates, trade and energy👇

1️⃣ **A 224-billion-parameter model comes to desktop computers**

Tencent has released open-source quantized weights and code for Hy4 flash, allowing the 224-billion-parameter model to run on a 64GB Mac mini. Official tests on hardware with 48GB of GPU memory also recorded speeds of up to 89 tokens/s.

The key takeaway: quantization is lowering the hardware requirements for running large models locally. Real-world performance will still depend on the device and task.

2️⃣ **AI server demand is strong, but tech stocks are under pressure**

AI-related stocks are selling off, putting the Nasdaq 100 at risk of entering a bear market. Meanwhile, Dell has raised its annual sales forecast again, driven by demand for AI servers.

Industry orders and stock valuations are moving at different speeds. Watch whether revenue meets expectations, how long investments take to pay off, and how much financing costs. South Africa’s central bank has also expressed concern about surging debt at major tech companies.

3️⃣ **Where are interest rates heading? Markets are hedging against two shifts**

Reports suggest U.S. PCE inflation pressures still support further Fed tightening, while bond traders are hedging against a “hike first, cut later” scenario.

The logic: inflation could drive rate hikes, while weaker growth could create pressure for cuts. Germany’s growth figures were also revised down following a sharp drop in investment, bringing the economic recovery into sharper focus.

To understand the macro picture, watch prices and business investment together.

4️⃣ **Tariffs and chip controls are reshaping purchasing and supply chains**

The U.S. reportedly plans additional tariffs on several economies, including China, the EU, Japan and South Korea. The actual cost impact will depend on tariff rates, the products covered, exemptions and legal developments.

Malaysia is also discussing advanced-chip restrictions with the U.S. and tightening controls, potentially affecting equipment purchases and project timelines for local data centers.

Businesses will need to weigh purchase prices, delivery times and compliance costs.

5️⃣ **Gulf shipping restrictions add another variable to energy costs**

Following attacks in the Gulf, ADNOC has restricted access for certain oil tankers to waters near some of the UAE’s main oil and gas export hubs.

Continued disruption to loading, unloading and shipping schedules could put pressure on freight rates, insurance premiums and energy prices. The impact will depend on the scope and duration of the restrictions.

📌 One connection to remember today: **Technology is lowering barriers to adoption, while changes in interest rates, tariffs and shipping are affecting the cost of capital and goods.** Watch how these shifts feed through to corporate earnings and everyday prices.
