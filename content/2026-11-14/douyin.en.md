The AI boom has reached its moment of reckoning.

Today’s key takeaway: how far AI companies can go depends on whether their investments pay off—and whether they can keep security vulnerabilities under control.

The Federal Reserve’s latest Financial Stability Report now includes risks tied to AI and high valuations. If profits lag behind capital spending, an asset price correction could intensify funding pressure.

And that spending keeps climbing. JPMorgan expects AI infrastructure spending, combined with refinancing needs, to push U.S. investment-grade corporate bond issuance to a record high in 2027. Access to funding, and how much it costs, will increasingly shape project timelines.

Beyond funding, security is adding to the bill. Cursor disclosed unauthorized use of keys and leaks of some credentials. Related malicious extensions may have reached as many as 5,000 users. A single credential leak can spread the risk much further.

So when assessing AI companies from here, watch three things: how long their investments take to pay off, how much financing costs, and whether they can keep user data safe.
