# Personal Daily | September 7, 2026

## Today's Take

> What truly matters today is that policy, capital, and technology supply are being repriced in tandem. Central-bank gold purchases, rising bond yields, and resilient risk assets show capital still wavering between hedging and risk-taking. Domestic computing infrastructure taking on hyperscale inference workloads—and Southeast Asia’s chip choices being shaped by geopolitics—suggest that AI competition is expanding beyond model capabilities to deployment costs, platform ecosystems, and policy boundaries. Efficiency gains in developer tools will also increasingly depend on adaptation to underlying compute infrastructure.
