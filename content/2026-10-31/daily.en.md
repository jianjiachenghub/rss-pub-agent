# Personal Daily Brief | October 31, 2026

## Today's Take

> AI's expansion is being tested on both product monetization and access to financing. Operating-system integration could broaden reach, while massive investment in computing capacity and mounting credit pressure raise the bar for revenue and efficiency. Over the coming weeks, whether funding keeps pace with spending commitments will shape diverging valuations across technology platforms and chipmakers. Energy export restrictions and shortfalls in exchanges' dedicated reserves also suggest that risk pricing needs to place greater weight on supply flexibility and the capacity to absorb losses.
