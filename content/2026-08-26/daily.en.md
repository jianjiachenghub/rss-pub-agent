# Personal Daily | August 26, 2026

## Today's Take

> Today's Take: Policy, capital, and compute are being repriced in tandem. Economic resilience is dampening expectations for monetary easing, while AI capital expenditure has entered the earnings-validation phase. At the same time, chipmakers are expanding into system-level capabilities and model competition is shifting toward agent-based products. In the next phase, markets will place greater weight on real demand, engineering efficiency, and platform control than on parameter counts and valuation narratives alone.

### Arm Bets on Agentic Computing With Its First In-House AGI Chip

**Event:** At Hot Chips 2026, Arm unveiled its first internally designed, mass-production AGI CPU. Built on TSMC’s N3P process with a dual-chiplet design, it features up to 136 cores and 100 billion transistors, with a TDP of 300W.

**Why it matters:** Arm is extending its competitive reach from IP licensing into finished chips, reshaping the supply landscape for cloud-based agentic computing. Its 136 cores, support for 6TB of memory, and CXL 3.0 also shift the competitive focus toward memory capacity, latency, and system-integration efficiency.
