**11.9｜Trade Reprieve Extended, Tech Stocks Under Pressure**

Today's key developments span trade, markets, energy and tech products 👇

🌏 **Importers, exporters and buyers get two more months to plan**
China will extend the suspension of its retaliatory measures against the U.S. by another two months, including keeping an additional 24% tariff on hold, extending the trade truce into January 2027.
That gives short-term purchasing, shipping and inventory planning a clearer footing. Long-term costs still depend on further negotiations.

📉 **Tech stocks face pressure as investors shift allocations**
Hedge funds are reportedly selling tech stocks at one of the fastest rates on record. Citi has downgraded U.S. equities and upgraded European equities, while foreign capital is flowing into India and U.S. Treasuries are finding support.
OpenAI's and Anthropic's fundraising valuations have reportedly also come under pressure. What to watch next: how market volatility affects corporate fundraising, and whether companies can keep investing.

🛢️ **Oil prices fall, but fuel supplies remain under pressure**
Expectations of progress toward peace in the Middle East have pushed oil prices to their lowest levels since August, potentially easing transport and manufacturing costs.
Meanwhile, Russia has restricted gasoline sales following attacks on its refineries. Crude prices and gasoline supplies need to be tracked separately; the key now is whether production capacity, supplies and shipping can remain stable.

📱 **Apple Intelligence filings completed for tablets and computers**
Regulatory filings for Apple Intelligence reportedly now cover two additional device categories—Apple tablets and laptops—further advancing the compliance groundwork for its rollout across devices in China.
If you're waiting to try it, watch for three details: when access opens, which features are supported and what operating system version is required.

🎨 **Seedream 5.5 launches, with potential gains for image workflows**
ByteDance says the new model generates images much faster than 5.0Pro. It is already available in Doubao, Doubao Work and Jimeng, with API access coming soon to Volcengine Ark.
For anyone creating illustrations or design drafts, the question is how much time it saves across repeated revisions. The available materials offer no quantified speed comparison yet, and developers will also need to watch API pricing and concurrency limits.
