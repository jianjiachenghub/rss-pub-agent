AI is still accelerating, but investors are hitting the brakes.

Today’s key takeaway: the AI industry faces a tougher test on returns. Technology is moving forward, but the spending has to add up.

Hedge funds are reportedly selling tech stocks at one of the fastest rates on record. That signals heavy short-term selling pressure, but it doesn’t yet mean AI demand is weakening.

Financing deserves even closer attention. Some reports warn that after roughly $1 trillion in debt-fueled expansion, AI companies could face an interest-rate shock in 2027. If borrowing costs rise, how quickly projects turn a profit becomes even more critical.

Products are still advancing. ByteDance has launched Seedream 5.5, claiming significantly faster image generation. Doubao, Jimeng, and other platforms have already integrated it. But concrete data on the speed gains and API pricing is still missing.

To track AI from here, focus on three questions: Are people using it? Will they pay for it? Can revenue sustain continued investment?
