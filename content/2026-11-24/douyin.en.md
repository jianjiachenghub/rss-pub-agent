AI is red-hot, but the stock has fallen below its IPO price.

Today’s key takeaway: The AI industry increasingly has to prove itself with tangible results.

OpenAI disclosed that proteins designed by GPT-6 showed evidence of binding to five targets in lab tests. That puts experimental evidence behind a general-purpose model’s scientific capabilities. But this is only an early signal. Effective drugs are still a long way off.

The consumer response is more direct: Best Buy raised its outlook on strong demand for computing devices and AI glasses. Demand for AI hardware is starting to shape how retailers see their business.

Meanwhile, Anthropic fell below its IPO price on just its fourth trading day. How much investors are willing to pay for expected growth is being put to the test. But pressure on the stock doesn’t yet mean its models are becoming less competitive.

Here’s what matters next for AI: Can progress in the lab and purchases by consumers gradually translate into business results that support valuations?
