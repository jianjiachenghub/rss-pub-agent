OpenAI disclosed that GPT-6 used reasoning and code to design proteins, with lab tests finding evidence of binding across 5 targets. General-purpose models’ research capabilities are now being tested experimentally. Potential use in medicines still requires validation of binding affinity, selectivity, and effects in the body.

Businesses face fresh uncertainty over purchasing, inventory, and pricing. Rates, product categories, and effective dates remain undisclosed, making the ultimate impact difficult to assess.

🏦 3. Calls for European rate hikes grow as Japan continues normalization
Two members of the European Central Bank’s Governing Council separately backed further rate hikes, citing concerns that inflation is spreading to more sectors. Their views still need to become formal policy through a council meeting. The Bank of Japan is reportedly set to continue normalizing interest rates in 2027, with the timing and scale still to be seen.

The former reflects consumers’ willingness to spend; the latter offers evidence from retail sales. The next test is whether spending holds up.

📉 5. Anthropic falls below its IPO price, putting its valuation to the test
On its 4th trading day, the stock fell below its $83 IPO price, erasing its gains since listing. Investors are becoming more cautious about what they will pay for expected growth. Upcoming earnings reports will help show whether the business can deliver.

💬 6. Doubao tests shopping, connecting chat to checkout
“Doubao Shopping” is being tested with a small group of users: AI helps compare prices and narrow down options, then directs users to Douyin Mall to place orders, with after-sales support integrated into the process. What matters next: accurate recommendations, a smooth purchase experience, and reliable after-sales service.

☁️ 7. Businesses get a more unified way to request model access
AWS opened the API for its Bedrock Trusted Access Program and launched public previews of Claude-related enterprise security and data control programs. Performance, security, and data management will all shape businesses’ model purchasing decisions. The exact scope still awaits further details.

🔩 8. Aluminum squeeze prompts exchange position-rule changes
The London Metal Exchange tightened rules on large positions in an effort to ease tight conditions in the aluminum market. Watch whether the changes ease the supply-demand imbalance and how prices respond.
