It’s time to run the numbers on the AI boom.

Today’s key takeaway: AI is gaining commercial traction, but how much profit growing demand delivers depends on whether transactions go through and costs can be recovered.

OpenAI has launched ChatGPT Wallet, letting agents make purchases with users’ explicit approval. AI assistants are moving into checkout, and their commercial value will depend directly on whether users grant permission and payments go smoothly.

There’s support on the infrastructure side, too: Cisco raised its sales outlook as AI drives demand for networking equipment. That shows AI spending is reaching a wider range of suppliers.

But expansion comes with a bill. Data center developer Nscale disclosed $5.5 billion in debt ahead of its IPO and still needs more cash. Even with strong demand, financing and debt repayments will affect how far growth can go.

So when assessing AI companies’ prospects, watch three things: whether users will pay, whether revenue can turn into profit, and whether there’s enough cash to fund expansion.
