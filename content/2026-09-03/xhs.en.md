Title: Today’s Must-Read Stories Go Beyond AI

September 3 was packed with news. I’ve condensed it into six key themes:

① Nvidia may acquire Hugging Face  
Multiple reports point to a deal worth around $13–14 billion, though nothing has been finalized. The real story isn’t the price tag—it’s that Nvidia could move beyond “selling GPUs” to gain greater control over open-source models, developer tools, and deployment channels.

② The AI copyright battle is escalating  
The U.S. government has backed OpenAI in its copyright dispute with news publishers, linking model training to national security. How AI companies use copyrighted content may soon become more than just a legal question.

③ The AI boom is beginning to reshape the broader economy  
Demand for computing power is widening the U.S. trade deficit. Meanwhile, Broadcom’s AI chip outlook fell short of expectations, sending its shares lower. The market is shifting from “anything AI-related goes up” to scrutinizing whether orders and growth can actually deliver.

④ Interest rates hold steady as market sentiment improves  
Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller signaled patience, lifting U.S. Treasuries and putting the S&P 500 on track for its biggest one-day gain in a month. Still, any rate cuts will depend on whether inflation continues to ease.

⑤ Energy and financial risks are rising  
War is putting pressure on fuel supplies, pushing U.S. diesel prices to a four-year high. Blackstone’s private credit fund has once again capped redemptions at 5%. One threatens transportation costs and inflation; the other exposes liquidity pressure in private markets.

⑥ Tech trends worth bookmarking  
Local inference tool Magnitude is reducing agents’ API costs and the risk of code leaving local environments. DeepMind is using hourly weather forecasts to enter power trading. Several pharmaceutical giants are backing Owkin’s efforts to shorten drug-development cycles with AI. At the same time, AI is finding vulnerabilities faster than financial institutions can fix them, while bioweapon risks are pushing up against new boundaries in safety review.

Today’s most important shift: AI’s impact has spread beyond model capabilities into capital markets, international trade, energy, security, and politics.

In one sentence: Nvidia is fighting to control the gateway to the AI ecosystem, while society as a whole is beginning to seriously calculate the cost of AI’s expansion.
