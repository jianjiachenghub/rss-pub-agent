The AI battle has reached the generators!

Today’s key takeaway: AI competition is expanding beyond model capabilities to power, construction, and commercial rollout.

According to the Financial Times, Anthropic has agreed to spend $3.4 billion on gas-powered generators. To expand computing capacity, AI labs are starting to foot the bill for power-generation equipment themselves. But how quickly data centers come online still depends on equipment deliveries and permits.

A similar expansion is underway in Brazil. ByteDance is seeking developers for a second data center phase, planned to be even larger than the $10 billion project already under construction. Turning those plans into actual computing capacity still requires construction and delivery.

Once the data centers are built, the models still need customers. China Mobile is partnering with Zhipu to bring GLM to its cloud platform, expanding access to enterprise customers. The partnership’s success will ultimately depend on paid usage and customer conversion.

So alongside model benchmarks, watch three things even more closely: whether the power is in place, whether projects get delivered, and whether customers are willing to pay.
