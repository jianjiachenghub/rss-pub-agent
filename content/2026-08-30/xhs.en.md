Title: Don’t Just Watch AI Today: Interest Rates, Baijiu, and Healthcare Are All Shifting

The key developments worth watching on August 30 can be grouped into four themes:

🤖 AI: More open models, longer-running agents

Zhipu AI has officially open-sourced GLM-5.3, a 743-billion-parameter MoE model supporting a 1-million-token context window. Open weights make it easier for enterprises to deploy long-running agents, but roughly 756GB of FP8 weights also show that the real barriers remain computing power and inference infrastructure.

OpenAI has reset usage limits for paid Codex and ChatGPT Work users. After fixing abnormal usage consumption, the same quotas are expected to support 10%–50% more work. It is also testing a “persistent mode” for Codex, enabling agents to proactively execute tasks over extended periods.

This means AI tools are evolving from “one-off Q&A” to “continuous work,” but costs, permission controls, and compounding errors will become increasingly critical.

💻 Coding tools: Stable model access is becoming a new risk

OpenAI announced that it would stop supplying models to Cursor, after which Anthropic said it would increase Claude compute support.

When choosing coding tools, developers will need to consider not only model capabilities, but also whether platforms can secure stable model access and whether partnerships may change after mergers or acquisitions.

💰 Macro markets: The pressure from high interest rates is not over

Following the Jackson Hole meeting, global markets remain focused on the Federal Reserve’s path for inflation, employment, and interest rates. Upcoming US jobs data will be a key test of whether the Fed can maintain its hawkish stance.

Meanwhile, rising bond yields have already added tens of billions to the debt-servicing costs of G7 countries. High rates are affecting more than stock valuations—they are materially squeezing governments’ fiscal room.

📊 Capital and consumption: The AI trade is cooling as traditional sectors diverge

Investors are beginning to reduce their concentrated exposure to the AI theme, with capital flowing into defensive sectors such as pharmaceuticals and lifting related stocks.

The consumer side remains challenging: Shanxi Fenjiu’s first-half revenue fell 12.18% year over year, while net profit dropped 24.29%. Simultaneous pressure on revenue and profit at a leading baijiu producer suggests that the recovery in premium consumption still needs to be watched.

🏗️ One more long-term theme: AI is becoming “heavy” again

a16z has launched the Machine Age Fund, focusing on investments in chips, networking, memory, cooling, and data centers. Capital’s attention is expanding beyond software models to electricity, thermal management, and capacity development.

The one takeaway worth remembering today:

AI is moving from a “model race” into an era of “infrastructure + persistent agents.” Meanwhile, high interest rates continue to constrain governments and businesses, while market capital is beginning to rotate out of the crowded AI trade and into defensive assets such as healthcare.
