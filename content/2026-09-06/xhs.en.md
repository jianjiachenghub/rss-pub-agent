# Today’s Must-Read: AI Is Starting to Deliver “Verifiable Results”

September 6 brought a flood of news. Beyond AI model upgrades, finance, interest rates, geopolitical conflicts, and gold trade routes are all shifting at the same time. Here are today’s six key themes👇

### 1️⃣ AI Is Entering the Era of “Verifiable Engineering”

Anthropic announced that Claude, led by Tianyi Peng, generated roughly 13 million lines of Lean 4 code and more than 30,000 intermediate theorems in 11 days, completing a formal proof of Fermat’s Last Theorem.

What truly matters is not that “AI can solve math problems,” but that it can turn human proofs into engineering artifacts that machines can verify line by line.

But this raises new questions: How can 13 million lines of code be compressed, maintained, and reviewed by humans? AI has lowered production costs, but pushed governance complexity to the next level.

### 2️⃣ General-Purpose Agents Are Facing Large-Scale User Testing

According to the daily report, GPT-6 Astra is rolling out to select paid users and API customers. It can operate browsers, update CRMs, generate personalized emails, and coordinate multiple agents to complete complex tasks.

The focus of industry competition is shifting from benchmark scores to:

- Whether agents can actually operate software
- How long they can work continuously
- Whether they can correct their own mistakes
- Whether humans can maintain oversight at all times

The faster their capabilities expand, the more critical access controls, cybersecurity, and end-to-end auditing become.

### 3️⃣ AI Skills Are Becoming an Entry Requirement in Finance

According to the Financial Times, UBS is requiring newly hired junior bankers to have AI expertise.

This may be a very practical signal for the job market: many roles may not be replaced directly by AI. Instead, their standards will first be redefined by people who know how to use it.

Meanwhile, traders are viewing Chinese stocks as a cheaper way to participate in the AI rally. The global AI trade is beginning to seek new, lower-valued markets.

### 4️⃣ AI Companies Will Soon Face the Hard Metrics of Public Markets

According to the daily report, Anthropic is expected to release its IPO prospectus in late September, with a roadshow potentially beginning as early as mid-October.

Once it enters the public market, attention will shift beyond model capabilities to questions such as:

- Whether revenue growth is sustainable
- How high inference costs really are
- Whether the customer base is overly concentrated
- Whether cash burn can be controlled

The prospectus could also become a new valuation benchmark for other foundation-model companies.

### 5️⃣ Global Interest Rates Continue to Diverge

The European Central Bank is expected to raise rates again, maintaining its hawkish stance. In the US, markets are awaiting inflation data to assess whether the Federal Reserve needs to tighten policy further this month.

For ordinary investors, the key is not predicting a single rate hike, but watching how long high rates will last and whether the policy gap between Europe and the US continues to widen.

### 6️⃣ Geopolitical Risks Are Reshaping Energy and Gold Routes

After Israel again carried out airstrikes on Lebanon, Lebanese officials warned that the situation could escalate dangerously. If the conflict expands, the geopolitical risk premium in energy prices may rise again.

Meanwhile, amid Western sanctions, large volumes of Russian gold are flowing through Hong Kong. The gold has not disappeared—it is simply finding new trade routes that bypass Western markets.

The one takeaway worth remembering today:

AI is moving from “demonstrating capabilities” to “operating in real-world systems,” while interest rates, wars, and trade routes continue to determine where capital ultimately flows.
