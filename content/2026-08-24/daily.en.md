# Personal Daily | August 24, 2026

## Today's Take

> Today's Take: The real story is the synchronized repricing of policy, capital, and computing power. Chip giants are competing to control access to open models, while major financing rounds continue to bet on AI infrastructure. Yet rising inventories, spiraling tool costs, and geopolitical trade friction are testing returns on investment. Risk appetite has not receded, but attention is shifting from scale to execution efficiency and cash returns.
