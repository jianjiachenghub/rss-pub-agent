# Personal Daily Brief | November 11, 2026

## Today's Take

> AI competition is expanding from model capabilities into office work, coding and end-to-end security workflows, making engineering efficiency a key test of product value. But further expansion increasingly depends on interest rates, how credit risk is shared and rules governing chip trade. Bond markets are under pressure just as lenders seek insurance to share the risks of financing compute capacity. Assessing tech platforms' growth now requires asking whether technical gains can cover financing costs—and who ultimately bears the risk.
