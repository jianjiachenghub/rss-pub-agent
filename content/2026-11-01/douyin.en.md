AI subscriptions? Time to crunch the numbers.

Today’s key takeaway: when choosing AI tools, look beyond model capabilities. Usage limits and platform rules matter too.

Starting in December, OpenAI plans to give ChatGPT and Codex a shared subscription allowance measured in tokens, with instant chats exempt. That means running complex agent tasks could eat into the allowance you have left for coding.

Meanwhile, OpenAI has reportedly cut its fundraising target from $30 billion to around $20 billion and lowered its valuation. But how much it plans to raise and how much actually comes in are separate questions.

Elsewhere, Claude Code now restricts recreating certain existing games, even under different names, while original development remains allowed. Even if model capabilities stay the same, platform rules can change whether a task can be carried out.

So before buying an AI subscription, check how often the allowance resets and how much extra usage costs. Then make sure you can use it for your most important tasks. All of this affects whether that subscription is worth the money.
