**11.1｜6 Key News Stories to Follow Today**

Today’s focus comes down to a few practical questions: how AI tools charge, where borrowing costs are heading, and whether there’s enough energy for winter👇

📱 **1｜ChatGPT and Codex plan to share usage limits**
Starting in December, OpenAI plans to give Plus, Pro, and Business users a shared, token-based usage allowance for ChatGPT and Codex. Instant chats will not count toward this allowance.

For people who use AI for both work and coding, complex tasks in one could eat into the allowance available for the other. When choosing a subscription, consider how quickly you use it up, when it resets, and what extra usage costs.

📉 **2｜Bond markets: watch both rates and risk appetite**
The Financial Times reports a sharp sell-off in junk bonds, with bond investors also staying on the sidelines ahead of the U.S. midterm elections. Separately, Bloomberg reports that a rebound in U.S. employment could support another Federal Reserve rate hike.

Together, these stories raise one question: when will borrowing costs come down? The timing remains uncertain. The summaries provide no specific figures for the declines or employment, so it’s too early to draw firm conclusions.

🔥 **3｜Winter energy: stockpiles and shipping under pressure**
Germany’s natural gas reserves have fallen short of their target. With a cold snap arriving, its supply buffer faces a test.

Meanwhile, Bloomberg reports that a Pakistani LNG tanker was attacked near the Strait of Hormuz, despite Iran having previously approved the shipment. Watch how long the cold lasts, how quickly reserves are replenished, and whether shipping routes remain safe.

🪙 **4｜Binance: asset replenishment still needs verification**
Binance says it has replenished roughly 90% of the affected assets, with full proof of reserves expected on November 3 (UTC). The remainder involves tokens with low liquidity, and the cost and timing of replacement purchases remain unclear.

Watch three things: when the remaining assets are replenished, what the full proof of reserves reveals, and whether withdrawals run smoothly. Bloomberg also reports that the FBI has attributed the theft of more than $6 billion to North Korea, though the provided excerpt includes no evidence supporting that attribution.

🎮 **5｜Claude Code: service rules also shape development workflows**
Anthropic has confirmed that Claude Code uses server-side rules to restrict recreations of specific existing games, even under different names. Original development based on your own designs, assets, or game genres remains allowed.

For game developers, model capability is only part of the picture. It also matters which tasks the tool permits and how much key workflows depend on it.

💰 **6｜OpenAI funding: watch how much actually comes in**
OpenAI has reportedly lowered its fundraising target from $30 billion to about $20 billion, and its proposed valuation from $1.4 trillion to $1.25 trillion.

That cuts the fundraising target by roughly one-third. What matters next is how much funding actually closes, when the money arrives, and how much research and computing spending it can cover.
