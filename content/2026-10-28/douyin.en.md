The next AI battle will be won on execution.

Today’s key takeaway: access to business workflows, compute, and electricity are becoming critical in the enterprise AI race.

OpenAI is expanding its partnership with ServiceNow, bringing models and agents into enterprise workflows—and connecting ChatGPT to those processes. The chat window is becoming a gateway to getting things done.

But running those workflows takes compute. OpenAI reportedly plans to buy Google’s AI chips, adding an option beyond Nvidia. Still, purchase volumes and delivery schedules remain unclear. It’s too early to say how much it could save.

And chips need electricity. The UK is tying data center grid connections to jobs and local investment. That means project timelines also depend on eligibility for grid access and commitments to invest locally.

So, to judge an AI company’s competitive strength going forward, look at how much real business it can handle—and what it actually costs to keep that business running.
