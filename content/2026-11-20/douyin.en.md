AI is accelerating. The stock market is hitting the brakes.

Today’s key takeaway: AI applications are moving forward, but the market is recalculating what AI is really worth.

Anthropic shares reportedly fell 8.5%, and the S&P 500 entered correction territory. Concerns over AI valuations are heating up.

But even as stocks cool, products keep advancing. Google’s Gemini can now operate a Mac on a user’s behalf. The race between AI assistants is moving from chat windows to computer desktops.

There’s also news on computing power: the U.S. has approved exports of more than 879,000 AI chips to China, with recipients including Alibaba and Tencent. But approval doesn’t mean delivery, and those chips can’t be directly counted as training capacity.

To gauge where AI stocks go next, watch three things: Are people using the features? When will the chips arrive? Can companies turn their spending into revenue?
